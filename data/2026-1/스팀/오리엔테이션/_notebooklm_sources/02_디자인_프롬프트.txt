The style must strictly follow a clean, minimal white-tone vector illustration aesthetic — like a well-designed SVG infographic. The background is a soft off-white paper tone (#FDFDFB) with generous whitespace and absolutely no texture, no gradient mesh, no photographic elements. All diagrams, matrices, graphs, and arrows are drawn as crisp flat vector shapes with uniform thin strokes (2-3px), rounded line caps, and no drop shadows. Node-and-edge network graphs use simple filled circles connected by straight thin lines. Matrices are rendered as clean bracketed grids with tabular, evenly spaced numbers.

The accent palette is limited to exactly four soft colors used sparingly against the white: dusty teal (#3E9C93) for the primary concept and headings, warm coral (#E8705A) for emphasis, warnings, and the isolated/lonely node, soft mustard (#E4B04A) for highlights and hub nodes, and slate navy (#33415C) for body text and matrix numbers. Never use more than three accent colors on a single slide. Large areas of pure white must remain empty.

Small, charming hand-drawn-feeling animal characters appear as light accents — a round fox, a rabbit, a bear cub, a cat, a small bird, a turtle — all drawn in the same flat vector style with simple dot eyes, minimal facial features, gentle rounded bodies, and no outlines heavier than the diagram strokes. These animals are STRICTLY SECONDARY decoration and must obey these rules: at most TWO animal characters per slide, each no taller than 15% of the slide height, always placed in the empty margins or beside a diagram — never overlapping text, numbers, or matrix cells, never centered as the main subject. When students or friends in a social network need to be represented, the animals may stand in as the network's nodes, drawn small and evenly spaced. The animals may hold a tiny speech bubble with a short phrase, but the bubble must never obscure the diagram.

The overall impression is a quiet, airy, modern educational poster: precise vector geometry carrying the mathematical content, with a few cute animal companions watching from the edges. Typography is clean and geometric, generous line spacing, left-aligned. Captured straight-on, perfectly flat, no perspective, no frame, no border.
